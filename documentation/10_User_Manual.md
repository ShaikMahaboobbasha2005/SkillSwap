# 10 — User Manual

## SkillSwap — How to Use the Platform

*Written for students, evaluators, and first-time users.*

---

## Getting Started

SkillSwap is a web application. Open it in your browser (Chrome, Firefox, or Edge recommended). The landing page will greet you if you are not yet logged in.

---

## 1. Registration

1. On the landing page, click **"Get Started"** or **"Sign Up"**.
2. Fill in your **Name**, **Email address**, and a **Password**.
3. Click **"Create Account"**.
4. You will be taken directly to your personal **Dashboard**.

> Your email is your unique identifier. Choose a password you will remember — there is no "forgot password" feature in the current version.

---

## 2. Logging In

1. Click **"Sign In"** on the landing page.
2. Enter your registered **Email** and **Password**.
3. Click **"Log In"** — you are taken to your Dashboard.

---

## 3. Setting Up Your Profile

A complete profile helps others trust you and find you through discovery.

### 3.1 Edit Basic Information
1. Click your **avatar** (top-right corner) → **"My Profile"**.
2. On your profile page, click **"Edit Profile"**.
3. Update your **Name**, **Location** (city only, e.g. "Hyderabad"), and **Bio** (max 160 characters).
4. Add your social links (LinkedIn, GitHub, Instagram, YouTube, Website).
5. Click **"Save Changes"**.

### 3.2 Upload a Profile Photo
1. On your profile page, hover over your avatar circle and click the **camera icon**.
2. Select an image (JPG, PNG, or WEBP, up to 10 MB).
3. Use the cropping tool to frame your photo.
4. Click **"Apply"** — your new photo is uploaded and saved automatically.

### 3.3 Upload a Profile Banner
1. Click the **"Edit Banner"** button (top-right of the banner area).
2. Select a banner image.
3. Crop and save as above.

---

## 4. Adding Your Skills

Skills are the core of SkillSwap. Add what you can teach and what you want to learn.

### 4.1 Add an Offered Skill (What You Can Teach)
1. On your profile page, find the **"Skills Offered"** section and click **"+ Add Skill"**.
2. Enter the **Skill Name** (e.g. "Guitar", "React", "Photography").
3. Choose a **Category** from the 20 available options.
4. Set your **Proficiency Level** (Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced / Expert).
5. Optionally add a short description and years of experience.
6. Click **"Save Skill"**.

### 4.2 Add a Wanted Skill (What You Want to Learn)
1. Find the **"Skills Wanted"** section and click **"+ Add Skill"**.
2. Follow the same steps, choosing what you want to learn.

### 4.3 Edit or Deactivate a Skill
- Click the **pencil icon** on a skill card to edit it.
- Use the **Active/Inactive toggle** to temporarily hide a skill from discovery without deleting it.
- Click the **trash icon** to permanently delete a skill.

---

## 5. Discovering Other Users

1. Click **"Discover"** in the navigation bar.
2. Browse the user cards — each card shows a person's name, location, rating, and top skills.
3. **Search** by skill name using the search bar.
4. Use **Filters** to narrow by:
   - Skill category (Programming, Music, etc.)
   - Skill type (Offer or Learn)
   - Proficiency level
5. Click on a user's card or name to view their full public profile.

---

## 6. Viewing Recommendations (Matches)

1. Click **"Matches"** in the navigation (or the orbit icon in the mobile bottom bar).
2. The system shows users whose skills complement yours — sorted by a **Compatibility Score** (0–100%).
3. Each match card shows:
   - **"They Can Teach You"** — their skills that match what you want to learn.
   - **"You Can Teach Them"** — your skills that match what they want to learn.
   - A percentage score and tier label (Excellent / Strong / Potential / Related).
4. For **AI-enhanced recommendations**, click **"✨ AI Recommendations"** — this uses Google Gemini to analyse deeper connections between skill categories.
5. Click **"View Profile"** to see the full profile, or **"Swap Skills →"** to send a request.

---

## 7. Sending a Swap Request

1. On a user's profile or recommendation card, click **"Request Swap"** or **"Swap Skills"**.
2. A modal appears:
   - **"I'll Offer"**: select one of your own offered skills.
   - **"I'm Requesting"**: select one of the other user's skills that you want to learn.
   - **Optional**: add a short message (max 500 characters) to introduce yourself.
3. Click **"Send Swap Request"**.
4. The other user is notified instantly. Your request appears in **"Swap Requests → Outgoing"**.

---

## 8. Responding to Incoming Requests

1. Click **"Swaps"** in the navigation.
2. Open the **"Incoming Requests"** tab.
3. Review the request — you can see what skill the other person is offering and what they want from you.
4. Click:
   - **"Accept"** — the swap is confirmed and chat becomes available.
   - **"Reject"** — the request is declined.

---

## 9. Using Chat

Chat is available once a swap request is accepted.

1. Go to **"Chats"** in the navigation (or Swaps → open an accepted swap → "Open Chat").
2. Your conversation list shows all active (accepted) swaps.
3. Click a conversation to open the chat workspace.
4. Type your message in the box at the bottom and press **Enter** or click **Send**.
5. Message status indicators:
   - ✓ Sent
   - ✓✓ Delivered
   - ✓✓ (green) Read
6. To **reply** to a specific message, hover over it and click the reply icon.
7. To **delete** your own sent message, hover over it and click the delete icon.

---

## 10. Scheduling a Video Call

1. In an active chat, click the **Video Call** (📹) icon in the top-right of the chat header.
2. Choose:
   - **"Start Video Call Now"** — launches an instant session immediately.
   - **"Schedule for Later"** — pick a date, time, duration (15/30/45/60 min), and optional topic.
3. A **meeting card** appears in the chat thread — both participants can see it.
4. When it's time, click **"Join Call Now"** on the meeting card.
5. A full-screen Jitsi Meet video call opens inside the app.
6. Click **"Leave Call"** (red button) to exit and return to chat.

> You will receive a notification 15 minutes before a scheduled session.

---

## 11. Completing a Swap

Once you have finished exchanging skills, mark the swap as complete.

1. Go to **Swap Requests** and find the accepted swap.
2. Click **"Mark Completed"**.
3. Your partner receives a notification and sees a banner asking them to confirm.
4. Your partner clicks **"Confirm Completion"**.
5. The swap is moved to **Swap History**, and both users' completed-swap counters increase.

> Both parties must confirm. If your partner clicks **"Not Yet"**, the swap returns to active status.

---

## 12. Rating and Reviewing Your Partner

After a swap is completed, you can leave a review.

1. In **Swap History**, find the completed swap.
2. Click **"Rate Partner"** (visible only for unrated completed swaps).
3. Select 1–5 stars.
4. Optionally write a review (max 500 characters).
5. Click **"Submit Rating"**.

Reviews appear on your partner's public profile and feed into their average rating.

---

## 13. Managing Your Portfolio

Show your work and build trust with a portfolio gallery.

### Upload a Portfolio Item
1. Go to **My Profile** → **Portfolio** section → click **"View Portfolio"** or **"+ Upload"**.
2. Drag and drop or select an image (JPG, PNG, WEBP ≤ 10 MB) or video (MP4, WEBM ≤ 50 MB, ≤ 60 seconds).
3. Add an optional caption (max 500 chars) and link it to one of your skills.
4. Click **"Upload"** — the item appears immediately in your grid while uploading.

### Edit or Delete
- Hover over a portfolio card → click the **pencil** (edit caption/skill) or **trash** (delete) icon.
- You have **5 seconds** to undo an edit or deletion — a countdown toast appears at the top of the page.

### React to Others' Work
1. Open any portfolio item (click to view lightbox).
2. Click **"✨ Appreciate"** to react with: 👍 Like, 🔥 Impressive, 👏 Great Work, or 💡 Creative.
3. Click the same reaction again to remove it.

---

## 14. Notifications

The **bell icon** (🔔) in the top navigation bar shows a badge when you have unread notifications.

- Click the bell to see recent notifications in a dropdown.
- Click any notification to jump directly to the relevant swap, chat, or profile.
- Click **"Mark all as read"** to clear the badge.
- Click **"View all notifications →"** to go to the full Notifications page (`/notifications`).

**Events that trigger notifications:**
- New swap request received
- Swap accepted or rejected
- Completion requested or confirmed
- Video meeting scheduled, started, or cancelled
- 15-minute meeting reminder
- New rating received

---

## 15. Settings and Appearance

1. Click your **avatar** (top-right) → **"Settings"**.
2. Under **Appearance**, choose your preferred theme:
   - 🖥️ **System** — follows your OS/browser dark mode setting.
   - ☀️ **Light** — always light mode.
   - 🌙 **Dark** — always dark mode.
3. The theme switches instantly and is saved to your browser.

---

## 16. Logging Out

1. Click your **avatar** (top-right corner).
2. Click **"Logout"** (shown in red at the bottom of the dropdown).
3. You are returned to the landing page and your session is cleared.
